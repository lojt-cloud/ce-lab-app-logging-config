Log Group: /aws/application/api
Retention: 30 days

Reason: 30 days is enough to debug recent issues and spot short-term trends,
without keeping data indefinitely and driving up storage cost. Default
retention (never expire) grows storage cost forever for no added benefit
in a lab/dev environment.

Set via:
aws logs put-retention-policy --log-group-name /aws/application/api --retention-in-days 30
